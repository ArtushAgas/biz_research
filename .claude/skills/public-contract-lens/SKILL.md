# Public-contract lens (NEN / VVZ / TED)

Czech public bodies (ministries, offices, courts, hospitals, schools, municipalities, regional and state companies) buy goods, services and works through NEN, with larger notices in VVZ and TED. For EVERY idea ask two questions: (1) can the public sector be a customer or a second channel? (2) does the public-contract evidence change the risk, margin or scale? Never assume yes: check. "Not applicable" and "not verified" are valid answers.

## Steps
1. **Translate the idea into search terms**: 2-4 Czech keywords (e.g. úklid, ostraha, údržba zeleně, IT podpora, překlady, stravování, tisk, dodávka X) and the CPV code(s) (cleaning 90910000/90919200, security 79710000, grounds 77300000, IT support 72250000/72500000, catering 55500000, printing 79800000; look up others on the CPV list).
2. **Search the portals.**
   - With the browser (local sessions): follow the `browser-harvest-playbook` skill (NEN keyword URL, detail pages, VVZ form search, TED notice).
   - Without a browser (agents, cloud): `WebSearch` with `site:nen.nipez.cz <keyword>`, `site:ted.europa.eu <keyword> Czechia`, `site:smlouvy.gov.cz <keyword>`, and Czech procurement news. If the portals cannot be read, write "NEN not verified" and list what to check; do not guess volumes.
3. **Tally** (state the date and the sample, e.g. "first 50 hits for 'úklid', 2026-10-28"):
   - number of open and recently awarded tenders; buyer types; Prague vs regions;
   - estimated and awarded values (TED notice or smlouvy.gov.cz; NEN lists rarely show values);
   - contract length, repeats by the same buyer, frameworks or DNS;
   - procedure: above-threshold (also on TED) vs small-scale / sub-threshold "otevřená výzva" (short deadlines, lighter requirements);
   - award criteria (price only vs price plus quality, wage floors, references) and number of bids; winners and whether SMEs win.
4. **Feasibility for this owner** (read `profile.md`): qualification and references needed; trade licence or s.r.o. fit; working capital (monthly invoicing, payroll before payment) vs the 100k EUR ceiling; staffing and part-time hours incl. bid-writing time; whether a newcomer without references can enter as a subcontractor, via sub-threshold calls, or not at all; margin at the winning prices (awarded prices are a hard price anchor).
5. **Classify**: `Primary channel` (public demand is the business) · `Secondary channel` (worth adding after launch) · `Subcontract only` · `Price/demand benchmark only` · `Not applicable` · `Not verified`.

## Output block (max ~120 words, tag every figure)
**Public-contract lens**: classification; keywords/CPV searched; sample tallied (n, date); typical value and length; buyer types; competition (bids, winners); entry barriers; feasibility for the owner (capital, references, hours); effect on the score (up/down/none) and why; URLs [sourced: URL, date] or [estimate: assumption].

## Cautions
- Public figures only; no names of private individuals (contact persons on tenders). Company names of winners and public buyers are fine.
- Research summary, not legal advice: public-procurement law (zákon 134/2016 Sb., ZZVZ), thresholds and qualification must be verified with a lawyer before bidding.
- Do not submit bids, register, or contact buyers as part of research.
- Public demand is not automatically good: price-only awards squeeze margins, wage-floor and reference criteria favour incumbents, and payment delays tie up capital. Say which applies.
