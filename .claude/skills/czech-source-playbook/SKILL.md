# Czech source playbook

Prefer primary sources, then reputable press. Always record URL and date. If a site blocks fetching, try the search engine's cached snippet, the site's open-data/API page, or a scraping/browser MCP if installed.

## Market and statistics
- CZSO (Czech Statistical Office, czso.cz) and DataStat: sector turnover, wages, household spending, population.
- Eurostat (ec.europa.eu/eurostat): cross-country comparison by NACE.
- Other markets: GUS (Poland), KSH (Hungary), Statistical Office of the SR (Slovakia), Destatis (Germany), INSEE (France), Statistik Austria.
- Google Trends (region CZ, and origin market) for demand signals.
- Industry associations, Czech Chamber of Commerce, CzechInvest, Česká národní banka reports, CPI and wage releases.

## Companies and competitors
- ARES (ares.gov.cz, has a public API): firm counts by CZ-NACE, founding dates, status.
- Justice.cz (obchodní rejstřík, sbírka listin): financial statements of s.r.o./a.s.
- Firmy.cz, Google Maps: local density and reviews.
- Živnostenský rejstřík: trade-license holders.

## Prices
- Heureka.cz, Zboží.cz: retail price ranges for goods. Note VAT included.
- Competitor e-shops and price lists; marketplaces (Alza, Mall, Allegro CZ, Aukro).
- Rent: Sreality.cz, Bezrealitky, commercial listings.
- Salaries and freelance rates: Jobs.cz, Prace.cz, Profesia.sk, LinkedIn, platform listings; CZSO wage statistics.

## Suppliers and sourcing
- Europages, Kompass, Alibaba/Made-in-China (check supplier verification), trade-fair exhibitor lists, national wholesaler directories.
- Customs/excise and tariff: TARIC, Celní správa.

## Law and regulation
- zakonyprolidi.cz and e-Sbírka for consolidated Czech law; EUR-Lex for EU law.
- businessinfo.cz (state export/import and business guidance), Živnostenský rejstřík, Celní správa (excise), SÚKL (medicines/devices), SZPI (food), ČOI (consumer inspection), ÚOOÚ (GDPR), Finanční správa (VAT, taxes).

## Digital and startup ideas
- Product Hunt, Indie Hackers, Crunchbase, Failory, open startup revenue pages; Czech: StartupJobs, Czechitas, CzechCrunch.

## Method
- Cross-check any key number with a second source. Label as [sourced: URL, date] or [estimate: assumption].
- Search in Czech and the source-market language, not only English.
