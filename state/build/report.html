Lookalike-domain and fake-shop takedown for Czech e-shops

Monitor lookalike domains of an e-shop, collect evidence, prepare takedown notices for registrar, host, Google and Meta. Search terms: falešné e-shopy, ochrana značky, monitoring doménových jmen.

55PassPilot-first variant ≈ 59, borderline Investigate. Confidence low to medium. Capex ≈ 6,500 EUR (4,000 to 10,000).
Screen score 59Hours: 8 to 12 pitched, about 11 at 15 shopsEntity: VAT-payer s.r.o.

Why Pass, in five lines

  • Clones are real and rising, but losses fall mostly on consumers and brand reputation. No source shows a small Czech shop paying for protection. Named victims (Sportisimo, Tescoma, Super Zoo) are large brands.
  • The cheap tier is crowded: Recon free / $24, StoreLock $4.99, Thicket $49, Tramatm $20 to $105. The 990 to 2,500 CZK price sits at or above the top.
  • "Near-zero cost" is half true. No .cz zone feed exists and .cz takedown needs a court or police order. Takedown labour is the real cost.
  • Filing notices for a client for pay runs into the advocacy monopoly (Act 85/1996) and false-notice liability. Letting the client file its own is workable but less valuable.
  • Base case: about 15 shops after 12 months gives about 235 EUR/month profit against the 1,000 EUR target [estimate].

Legal and regulatory

Research summary, not legal advice. Verify with a Czech lawyer and accountant.

  • High Advocacy monopoly (85/1996 §1 to 2): paid, recurring drafting or sending of legal notices for a client is a grey zone. Redesign so the client files in its own accounts and legal-effect items go to a partner advokát.
  • Medium-High False notices: DSA Art. 16 and 23 (suspension of unfounded notifiers), unfair competition §2976 to 2978 OZ, personality rights §81 OZ, defamation §184 TZ if a "fake" label is wrong. Mitigate with client indemnity, human review, neutral wording.
  • Medium GDPR for WHOIS/RDAP data of natural persons (legitimate interest, Art. 14, Art. 10, records).
  • Use the VAT-payer s.r.o.: 990 CZK gross is 818 net, 2,500 gross is 2,066 net. Consider a dedicated s.r.o. to ring-fence liability.
  • Checklist: advocacy opinion 1,500 to 4,000 EUR, insurance 300 to 1,000 EUR/yr [estimates].

Top risks

  • No proven willingness to pay among small shops.
  • Legal exposure for notice filing.
  • .cz takedown needs a court or police order; fakes sit on disposable foreign hosting.
  • Churn in quiet months with no clone.
  • Tramatm or a law firm adds automated e-shop monitoring.

Would change the verdict

  • At least 5 shops pay 1,500+ CZK/month or 4,900+ CZK one-off, without discount.
  • Tramatm confirms it does not scan lookalikes and will refer or white-label.
  • An advokát confirms the evidence-pack model is outside the monopoly.
  • A white-label partner quotes under about 300 CZK per case.
  • NEN/VVZ shows recurring small brand-monitoring calls.
