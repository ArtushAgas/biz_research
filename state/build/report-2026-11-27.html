Legal and regulatory (research summary, not legal advice)

  • Trade: "Reklamní činnost, marketing, mediální zastoupení" is volná živnost (about 40 EUR); licence fit not scored per profile.
  • VAT: B2B service to German firms is reverse charge (Directive Art. 196; §9 ZDPH). Use the VAT-payer s.r.o.: 0% CZ VAT invoice with USt-IdNr, VIES check, souhrnné hlášení within 25 days after month end. Non-payer s.r.o. is a poor fit (identifikovaná osoba for Google Ads purchases, threshold). Google invoicing entity unverified (believed Google Ireland).
  • Outreach: keep to inbound search ads plus opt-in forms. UWG §7(2) Nr. 2 has no B2B carve-out for email; B2B phone needs at least presumed consent; Czech 480/2004 fines up to 10M CZK. Cold outreach is effectively off the table.
  • GDPR (lead forms, Art. 28 DPA, privacy link), UWG §5/§8 liability for client claims, German Impressum (DDG §5). Contract: client-owned Google Ads account, warranties, liability cap, indemnity.
  • Lawyer/accountant to verify (about 800-2,000 EUR one-off): governing law of outreach, controller/processor, liability, exact VAT wording, non-payer treatment, Google invoicing entity.
  • Regulatory burden Medium (inbound only); High if cold outreach.

Top risks

  1. Czech firms will not pay retainer plus ad budget; long sales cycles.
  2. RFQ volume/quality too low (German CPC 5-15 EUR, sometimes above 50; 10-25% qualify), causing churn.
  3. No references against agencies that have them.
  4. Legal exposure from lead-form personal data and misleading claims; temptation to use cold outreach.
  5. Labour ceiling (3-4 clients), low hourly value, not scalable.

What would change the verdict

  • At least 3 of 20 pitched firms sign a paid pilot (500-1,000 EUR setup plus 2,000 EUR or more ad budget) or a written LOI.
  • Keyword Planner shows meaningful German volume at CPC at or below about 4 EUR ("CNC Lohnfertigung", "Blechbearbeitung Tschechien").
  • A pilot delivers at least 3 qualified RFQs in about 90 days at 500 EUR or less per qualified lead.
  • NEN/VVZ/CzechTrade shows recurring small export-marketing calls (not verified).
  • A trade body or cluster offers a group channel to exporters.

Reshaped variant: pilot-first RFQ funnel with hybrid fee (score 59)

Fixed-price 90-day pilot (setup 500-1,000 EUR plus 2,000-3,000 EUR ad budget), then base 400-600 EUR/mo plus a fee per qualified RFQ. Productised technical RFQ page (drawing upload, ISO sections, 24 h reply) for CNC, sheet-metal and welding; target firms exporting 30%+ without direct buyers. Shortens first sale and ties fee to results, but lowers base fee and needs an auditable definition of "qualified".

30-day validation plan (budget under 200 EUR, about 15 hours)

  1. Days 1-3: Keyword Planner on 10-15 German terms (Lohnfertigung, Zerspanung, CNC-Fräsen, Blechbearbeitung, Schweißen, with Tschechien/Böhmen). Kill if median CPC above about 8 EUR and volume thin.
  2. Days 3-10: list 40 Czech subcontractors (10-100 employees) from ARES, CzechTrade, Auftragsbank; note who advertises in German.
  3. Days 7-20: pitch 20 with a free one-page audit of their German web presence (Czech-to-Czech outreach; confirm 480/2004 rules); offer the 90-day pilot. Target 3 paid pilots or LOIs and 6 discovery calls.
  4. Days 10-25: ask 2-3 prospects what they pay for fairs/portals, close rate, value of a won order (CZK willingness-to-pay anchor).
  5. Days 15-30: short accountant consult (reverse charge, Google invoicing entity) and lawyer consult (inbound-only compliance); full review only after a pilot signs.
  6. Day 30: proceed only if keywords are viable and at least 2 firms commit to paid pilots.
