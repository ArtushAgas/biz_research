## How popular would it be in Czechia?
- **Honey is very popular; the spoon format would probably be a small niche.** Consumption ~0.7-1 kg per person per year, ~7-10 kt in total, over 65,000 beekeepers. Czechs buy honey as a local, trusted product and compare it per kilogram, not per portion. Self-sufficiency fell from 141% (2011) to 67% (2024).
- **No Czech shop appears to sell honey spoons, sticks or straws** (Zboží, Dr. Max, Slevomat, beekeeper shops, Firmy.cz, Rohlik, Kaufland; Heureka, Amazon.de and Allegro could not be queried). The gap is as likely to mean low demand as an open opportunity. Only boxed portion honey exists as a benchmark (Hellma EUR 0.17, Breitsamer EUR 0.22 per portion).
- **Evidence strength: weak.** No Google Trends, Sklik, Heureka or Amazon.de data was reachable. "Popularity" rests on category size plus absent supply, which is inference. Expect a gift/novelty product with Q4 and January peaks, not daily use.

## Why
1. Category demand is large, but the spoon format has no proven Czech demand; substitutes are a jar plus a spoon and HoReCa portion cups at 1.4-6 CZK.
2. **The plastic spoon as briefed likely hits Act 243/2022 §6** (ban on Part A single-use plastics, which lists cutlery incl. spoons; fines up to CZK 5M), confirmed by the judge on zakonyprolidi.cz. The MŽP FAQ (2021, draft stage) says cutlery packed with food is in scope. Wooden or paper spoons fall outside the plastic definition. Whether a honey-filled spoon counts as cutlery or packaging still needs an MŽP written view.
3. A compliant variant looks feasible but no supplier or co-packer is confirmed (no EU white-label maker found; Asian minimum orders conflict at 1,000 / 20,000 / 200,000).
4. Gross margin looks fine at 50-70% on the e-shop price, but after customer acquisition (20-40%), shipping, breakage and ~EUR 250/month fixed costs contribution is ~EUR 0.10-0.20 per spoon. EUR 1,000/month needs ~8,300 spoons a month (~830 ten-packs), far beyond a niche. The competition report's "2,000 spoons a month" used a 12 CZK margin that ignores acquisition and shipping.
5. Capital is small, so a cheap test is rational. It is not a pursue case.

## Supply and legal highlights
- Landed cost: Asia small batch ≈ EUR 0.62; Asia large order ≈ 0.29; Czech co-packer with wooden spoon ≈ EUR 0.30-0.40 [estimate]. HoReCa cafés pay portion-cup prices, so B2B only works as a premium gift product (EUR 0.40+ per spoon).
- Needed for the variant: SVS food-business notification, Czech labelling and origin per Decree 490/2025 (in force 14.6.2026), no health claims, food-contact declarations (Reg. 1935/2004, 10/2011), packaging EPR (Act 477/2001) and EU PPWR (from 12.8.2026; a 2030 restriction on single-use plastic portions may touch honey portions), 12% VAT on honey. Entity: the VAT-payer s.r.o. Non-EU honey adds certificates, listed establishments and adulteration risk.

## Top risks
1. Plastic-spoon ban (up to CZK 5M) and unresolved classification of other variants.
2. No proven demand; may be seen as a gimmick next to a jar and spoon.
3. Acquisition cost on a low-ticket product eats the margin; sticky goods raise breakage and complaints.
4. No confirmed co-packer; crystallisation, 3-10% seal leakage and honey adulteration enforcement make supplier quality critical.
5. Food-regulation workload for an owner with no food background; honey is a trust/local category.

## What would change the verdict
A Czech co-packer quote under ~CZK 8 per finished spoon at MOQ 2,000-5,000; test shops converting at 2%+ on a 10-pack at 20 CZK with acquisition cost under EUR 2 per order; two or more cafés, tea shops or corporate buyers committing to 500+ spoons a month at EUR 0.45+; a written MŽP or lawyer view that plastic is allowed; Keyword Planner or Sklik data showing real volume for "medové tyčinky" or "medová lžička".

## 30-day validation plan (budget about EUR 150-400)
- **Week 1:** email the Ministry of Environment about plastic versus wooden spoons; ask a lawyer/accountant the open questions (cutlery vs packaging, wooden spoon and EPR, SVS notification for a pure reseller); request quotes from Beedol, Medokom and 3-5 other Czech beekeepers/packers for co-packed wooden spoons or sticks (price, MOQ, honey type, shelf life, certificates); pull Keyword Planner and Sklik volumes for "medové tyčinky", "medová lžička", "porcovaný med".
- **Weeks 1-3 (EUR 50-100):** landing page with mock-up photos and a pre-order or waitlist; ~14 days of Google Ads. Success: 150+ clicks and at least 3% sign-ups or 10 genuine pre-orders at 20 CZK a spoon; test a 10-pack and a gift box.
- **Weeks 2-4 (EUR 50-150):** 20-30 hand-filled wooden-spoon samples for about 30 Prague cafés, tea shops and corporate-gift buyers (check first whether free samples count as food production). Success: 5+ replies and 2+ commitments.
- **Day-30 gate:** go only if quotes meet the cost target and pre-orders or B2B commitments hit the thresholds; otherwise stop with under EUR 400 spent.
