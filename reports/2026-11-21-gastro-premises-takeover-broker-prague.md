## 2. Why, in 5 lines
1. Capital and hours fit. Demand exists but is mostly unbrokered; volume (500-1,000 Prague transfers/yr) is an estimate; no sourced fee schedule.
2. It is a licensed activity: brokering a lease of a nebytový prostor is realitní zprostředkování under 39/2020 Sb. (vázaná živnost, liability insurance, bezúhonnost). The owner's qualification is unknown; without it only the reshaped variant is open.
3. 25k CZK/month needs about 5 deals/yr at 77k margin or 8 at the 45k base margin. Realistic year 1: 2-4 deals, about +6k CZK/month base, a loss in the low case.
4. Fee model inconsistent: 3-5% of a 1M odstupné is 30-50k, below the 65-100k flat fee; the one datapoint is 65k on 1M (6.5%).
5. Commission bypass has no statutory protection; generalist agencies and free portals already compete; willingness to pay unvalidated.

## 4. Concept
Google Ads/SEO toward exiting tenants ("prodej restaurace / odstupné / převod nájmu"), free valuation, mandate, vetted buyer list; success fee 65-100k CZK flat or 3-5% of odstupné. Foreign model: HOGAPAGE (DE), licensed broker, success-only fee, listing packages 449 / 2,490 EUR; it brokers equipment/business, not leases (material difference). HOGAMAKLER existence unverified. Gastro churn: CZ 1,419 founded / 630 dissolved in 2024; 1,184 / 491 to 3 Oct 2025 [sourced: D&B via Seznam Zprávy, 2025].

## 7. Legal and regulatory (research summary, not legal advice)
- 39/2020 Sb. §2 a, §2 d bod 2: brokering a right to use a nebytový prostor is realitní zprostředkování [sourced: https://www.zakonyprolidi.cz/cs/2020-39, 2026-11-21]. Vázaná živnost with odborná způsobilost; object in Commercial Register for an s.r.o. (27 Cdo 3549/2020, not verified by judge).
- §7: liability insurance min 1.75M / 3.5M CZK, filed with MMR within 10 working days; fine up to 1M CZK without it (not verified by judge); premium about 2.5-5k CZK/yr [snippet/estimate].
- §5-6 bezúhonnost of broker, UBO, statutory bodies. §4 no escrow except bank/notary/lawyer. §§12, 17-19 consumer-oriented; restaurateur likely not a consumer. No statutory anti-bypass: use contractual tail clause.
- VAT: business transfer outside VAT; broker fee taxable 21%; VAT on odstupné contested. Recommended entity: the VAT-payer s.r.o.; non-VAT only for equipment-only flow under 2M CZK.
- AML: lease brokering obliged only at rent of at least 10,000 EUR/month; AMLR from 2027-07-10. GDPR and Act 480/2004 for lead emails; scraping listings uncertain.
- Equipment-only route possibly volná živnost "Zprostředkování obchodu a služeb"; mixed package is a gray zone.
- Burden: Medium-High. Items for lawyer/accountant: mixed-package scope, VAT on odstupné, bypass enforceability, qualification route.

## 9. Top 5 risks
1. Licence gate (odborná způsobilost). 2. Fee unvalidated, flat vs percentage conflict. 3. Bypass. 4. Thin deal flow, long cycles, landlord can block. 5. Tax/legal uncertainty.

## 10. What would change the verdict
Licensed partner or own qualification confirmed (total about 55-57); 5+ sellers paying 65k+ in the test (about 62-65); fee schedules from 3+ agencies; CPC at or below 20 CZK with lead-to-mandate above 10%; lawyer confirms VAT and tail clause.

## 11. 30-day validation plan (15-30k CZK, no licence needed)
1. Week 1: check qualification route (Annex 2 Trade Act, e-Sbírka); ask one licensed gastro-active broker about referral/co-broker split; one-hour lawyer/accountant call.
2. Weeks 1-2: anonymous fee quotes from 5-8 agencies and 5 sellers; record 20+ live odstupné prices; check Facebook groups by hand.
3. Weeks 2-3: landing page with free valuation form, 10-20k CZK ads; measure CPC, leads, real sellers. Do not broker specific leases without a partner.
4. Weeks 3-4: contact 10-15 café/restaurant operators about a buyer list.
5. Day-30 rule: proceed only with at least 5 seller leads, 2 willing to sign a mandate at 65k+, a licensed route and CPC at or below 30 CZK.

**Reshaped variant** (about 57, Pass, Low confidence): owner runs funnel, valuation tool and HOGAPAGE-style listing portal (449 / 2,490 EUR packages); a licensed broker handles the lease part for a referral share, or equipment/goodwill-only deals.
