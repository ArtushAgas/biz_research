# Korean-style unmanned photo booth, Prague (korean-photo-booth-studio-prague)
Date 2026-11-08 | Category local-physical | Screen 52 | **Final 57 (Pass as specced); reshaped rev-share pilot + event rental variant 60 (borderline Investigate, pilot only)** | Confidence Low
Capital: ~12-16k EUR for 1 unit incl. setup + 3 months runway (2 units ~25-30k); owner 4-6 h/week + 2-4 h/week outsourceable servicing. Public-contract lens: Not verified, probably not applicable (no NEN/TED run).

## Why, in 5 lines
1. Small capital, high contribution per session (~3.6 EUR, ~87%); success hinges on sessions/day at a low-rent site.
2. At base costs 1,000 EUR/month needs ~18 sessions/day (~530/month); Prague demand is unmeasured. Photoism single-machine franchisees abroad "may not earn" [sourced: eu.36kr.com/en/p/3735354850722050].
3. No Korean-format booth found in Prague or in Warsaw/Vienna/Berlin/Budapest; Life4Cuts seen in Paris, London, Melbourne, Singapore, event stands in Stockholm/Tokyo. Absence may mean weak CEE demand, not an open gap.
4. No moat: commodity hardware, unprotected format, licensed K-pop frames (the real draw) not legally usable without licences.
5. Downside capped by a cheap pilot (~1-3k EUR) before any hardware or lease.

## Legal (research summary, not legal advice)
Free trade "Fotografické služby" incl. self-service photo machines. EET 2.0 from 1 Jan 2027 (passed 9 Sep 2026, presidential signature reported 17 Sep, Sbírka number unconfirmed); open whether unmanned booth gets vending exemption. GDPR/minors (start-button consent, <15 guardian notice, fast deletion, DPA). IP: avoid Life4Cuts/Photoism names, K-pop props need licences. CE/LVD, HS code unknown (BTI ruling). Card-only vs cash risk (ČNB vs MF). Mall fire approval; street kiosk needs Prague public-space permit. Recommended entity: VAT-payer s.r.o. Needs lawyer/accountant verification.

## Reshaped variant (score 60, Investigate, pilot only)
Place 1 unit in venues free of rent for 25% of gross, rent it for events (6-12k CZK) when idle. Fixed ~150-300 EUR/month; break-even ~15/day for 1,000 EUR, but at 8/day still ~+460/month before events. Overlaps with entrenched rental firms.

## What would change the verdict
Up: pilot ≥15 sessions/day at rent <500 EUR/month or rev-share; CE-compliant unit quote 5-7k EUR with owned software; licensed frame deal. Down: pilot <8/day; chain announcing Prague/Vienna; EET/customs cost surprises.

## 30-day validation plan (<1,500 EUR; no hardware or lease before step 4)
1. Wk1: scout 8-10 sites (Palladium, Kampus, Chodov, Fashion Arena, student areas, cinemas, cafes), count 15-25-year-old footfall, ask for rate cards/turnover terms, 500-1,000 CZK Instagram poll, Maps/Instagram check.
2. Wk1-2: quotes from 3 OEMs (price, software ownership, CE/EMC docs, frame editor, payment, lead time); ask used-booth seller (mudah.my) for specs.
3. Wk2-3: rent an existing booth/trial unit for 2-3 weekend days at a cafe or student event under revenue share; count sessions and willingness to pay 5 EUR.
4. Wk3-4: pitch 3 venues for a 3-month rev-share trial (letter of intent, not lease).
5. Go/no-go at day 30: buy one unit only if pilot ≥15 sessions/day annualised, OEM quote ≤9k EUR with CE documents, and site at ≤900 EUR/month or rev-share.
