Why, in five lines

  1. The margin is not there. Base gross on the kit is about 65 EUR (14%) at 13,500 CZK. Lidl and Kaufland anchor the bare kit at 8,000 to 10,000 CZK.
  2. Paperwork is a weaker selling point than assumed. Today's route is a simple contract application (vyhláška 16/2016 §16), not a notification, and it disappears if the simplified regime passes. The real pain is zero-export hardware, the meter swap and SVJ or landlord consent.
  3. Demand is unproven. There is no Czech unit count. One seller reported about 20 kits a month in 2023. The closest earlier attempt (Be4You on Donio, July 2024) raised 0 of 300,000 CZK.
  4. Regulation is heavy as briefed. Importer duties under GPSR, WEEE, product liability and an open question whether an 860 Wp panel set counts as "above 800 W". Stock bought now sits through winter and can strand if the law changes.
  5. A no-stock variant fits you (Google Ads, shop building, building network) and costs under 10k EUR, but earns little per customer. It is a test, not yet a business.

Legal and regulatory

The legal report corrected the scout. Burden: High as briefed, medium for pure retail. Research summary, not legal advice.

What the law says

  • Simplified connection (vyhl. 16/2016 §16) is a contract application with 0 kW reserved capacity, not a notification.
  • Revision report and impedance measurement apply only above 800 W installed. An 860 Wp panel set may count as above 800 W, so ask PRE in writing before buying stock.
  • Export to the grid must be technically prevented. Cheap German-style kits may not connect as sold.
  • Prague is mostly PREdistribuce; it needs no impedance measurement or A1 document up to 800 W with zero export.
  • Amendment 1054 (free e-notification) failed in the Chamber in July 2026. Its current status is unconfirmed; check psp.cz tisk 67.

What you would need

  • Free trades: retail (item 48), brokerage (47), consultancy (60). Installing is a regulated craft trade, so refer it out.
  • GPSR importer or distributor duties, CE file, WEEE registration, product-liability insurance before 9 Dec 2026.
  • VAT 21% appears to apply to kits and installation (accountant to confirm).
  • Use the existing VAT-payer s.r.o. The non-VAT one would lose input VAT and trip the 2M CZK and 326,000 CZK limits.
  • GDPR roles and a power of attorney for the paperwork service.

Top risks and what would change the verdict

Top 5 risks

  • Price war. A 14% gross absorbs almost no surprise.
  • Regulatory swing. If the simplified regime passes, paperwork revenue vanishes. If it fails, the market stays small.
  • Compliance and liability. Importer duties, the 800 W ambiguity, fire or injury claims aimed at the seller.
  • Unproven demand. No Czech volumes; the one comparable service launch got zero pledges.
  • Third parties. Installers, distributors and SVJs decide the customer's outcome.

What would change my mind

  • A written quote at or below 230 EUR landed for a 2×400 Wp kit at 40 pieces, with CE and grid-code certificates.
  • Written PRE confirmation that the kit plus a named export limiter connects with no revision.
  • At least 8 paid deposits for a 2,500 CZK pack at 30 EUR or less per lead.
  • An installer paying 75 EUR or more per referral.
  • Passage of the simplified regime (pivot to accessories) or its final rejection (compliance-pack niche only).
