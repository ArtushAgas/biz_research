## Why, in five lines
1. Market already served: Brewive (Prague; model finder, exploded diagrams for 900 models, 5,000+ parts, dealer offer), Elektra Dvořák (100,000+ parts, next-day), Nadeta (~2,690 small-appliance parts), manufacturer shops, Kaufland.cz sellers [sourced: brewive.com, elektra-dvorak.cz, nadeta.cz, 2026-11-18].
2. The brief's order maths treats revenue as profit: 170 orders x ~400 CZK = 68k CZK revenue; at 25-35% net gross after shipping, fees, returns = 17-24k CZK gross before ads/fixed costs [estimate].
3. Best-selling seals sell at 29-99 CZK at specialists: single-digit CZK margin per order.
4. Supply unverified: no wholesale price list, MOQ or reseller programme found (REPA Group is an authorised EU distributor for De'Longhi/Kenwood/Braun/Ariete, no CZ entity, terms unknown).
5. No demand tailwind: small kitchen appliances are not in Annex II of Directive 2024/1799 [sourced: eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2024/1799, 2026-11-18]; tisk 62 passage/Senate dates not independently confirmed.

## Legal (research summary, not legal advice)
Burden Medium. Volná živnost sale of goods suffices; repair needs a regulated trade. 14-day withdrawal (§1829 OZ), 2-year liability, withdrawal button (CZ transposition unclear), LVD NV 118/2016, GPSR, RoHS, food-contact Reg 1935/2004 declarations, WEEE 542/2020 (parts-only producer status unverified), packaging EPR, trademark: no logos on compatible parts (CJEU Audi v GQ), "compatible with" wording only, never claim OEM. EUR 3 duty per item line on <=150 EUR consignments 1 Jul 2026-1 Jul 2028. Recommended entity: VAT-payer s.r.o. Flag for lawyer/accountant: WEEE status, LVD for parts, trademark clearance, withdrawal button.

## Top risks
No wholesale access at 30%+; low ticket size; price transparency (2-3x spread); incumbent response (Brewive adding non-coffee); 150-300 h catalogue build and fitment-guarantee returns.

## What would change the verdict
Written reseller quote >=35% gross on OEM jars/blades/brew groups at MOQ <=100; verified basket >=600 CZK; >=3,000 monthly non-coffee searches at CPC <5 CZK; dealer/white-label deal with Brewive or Nadeta; evidence small kitchen appliances come under ecodesign repairability before ~2028.

## 30-day validation plan (<700 EUR)
1. Wk1: Keyword Planner volumes/CPC for 40 non-coffee queries and top 30 models; skim Heureka/Kaufland bestsellers (free).
2. Wk1: email REPA, Brewive, BSH CZ, Philips CZ, Nadeta for reseller terms/MOQ (free).
3. Wk2: order 5-10 aftermarket samples (100-200 EUR); confirm whether Elektra Dvořák 29 CZK is VAT incl.
4. Wk2-3: zero-stock landing page/Shoptet trial, 20-30 SKUs, "compatible with" wording (100-200 EUR).
5. Wk3-4: 300 EUR Google Ads; measure CPA, add-to-cart, basket; accountant check on VAT/WEEE (200 EUR).
6. Wk4 go/no-go: go only if contribution before ads >=6 EUR, CPA <=4 EUR, basket >=500 CZK and one OEM source gives written >=35% terms.
