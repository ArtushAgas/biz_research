# Robot-vacuum and coffee-machine parts e-shop (CZ): decision report

- **Idea:** `robot-vacuum-parts-repair-shop-cz`, a Spareka-style Czech parts e-shop (model finder, Czech repair videos, opt-in replacement reminders) for robot vacuums and automatic coffee machines.
- **Category:** ecommerce-import
- **Date:** 2026-10-27
- **Verdict as briefed: PASS, 57/100.** Confidence Low-to-Medium: almost every economic input is an [estimate] or a search snippet.
- **Reduced variant** (robot-vacuum non-electrical compatible kits only): **61.5/100, INVESTIGATE**, Low confidence. It is worth a 30-day cheap test, not a build.
- **Capital:** as briefed about 28k EUR base cash need (22-36k) [estimate]; reduced variant about 10-16k EUR [estimate]. Both are well under the 100k EUR cap.
- **Hours/week:** 12-18 at launch, 9-12 stable (reduced variant 8-10), only if a 3PL packs orders and support is templated [estimate].
- Legal content is a research summary, not legal advice; regulated items need lawyer/accountant verification.

## 1. Why, in five lines
1. The business is real and low-capital. Wear parts have recurring demand and the owner has the skills (online shops, Google Ads, logistics, cleaning network).
2. The scout's "170-250 orders/month for 1,000 EUR" ignores customer acquisition cost. With realistic ad cost it is about 280 orders/month (9/day) in the base case [estimate].
3. A price war is the default outcome. Kaufland.cz kit sellers, Plus.Parts, Alza/Datart originals and several coffee-parts specialists already serve the market. Realistic compatible-kit prices (15-19 EUR incl. VAT) cut the margin from "75-79%" to about 35% after fulfilment.
4. The "Spareka proof" is French. Czech demand evidence is thin: no Czech search volumes, CPCs or competitor revenues.
5. The coffee half (descaler, brew groups, restricted originals) adds regulatory and supply burden with the thinnest margin. Drop it at launch.

## 3. Concept and why now
- Czech Spareka-style shop for robot-vacuum and coffee-machine wear parts (brushes, filters, mop pads, brew groups, gaskets). Online and CZ-wide; Prague only matters for the owner's location.
- Robot-vacuum installed base is growing: Roborock overseas revenue +63.5% in 2025 [sourced: https://newsroom.roborock.com/gl/news/roborock-reports-56-51-revenue-growth-in-2025-q1-2026-revenue-up-23-31-, 2026-10-27]. This is global, not Czech.
- The EU 3 EUR duty on low-value parcels (from 2026-07-01 to 2028-07-01) penalises dropshippers and favours bulk importers [sourced: commission.europa.eu news 2026-06-29, via search].
- Caveat: right to repair mainly obliges manufacturers, and brands may restrict parts supply, so it is weak support, not a driver.
- Origin proof: Spareka (FR) raised 11 MEUR (2023) and 25 MEUR (Jan 2025) [sourced: https://www.maddyness.com/2025/01/09/spareka-plateforme-leader-daide-a-la-reparation-leve-25-millions-deuros-et-ouvre-son-capital-au-grand-public/]. The 2020 round appears to be 5 MEUR (maddyness.com/2020/04/15), not 12 MEUR as stated in the brief; the 10 MEUR 2018 revenue figure is unverified.

## 5. Supply
- Compatible kits: Shenzhen/Guangdong accessory makers (Alibaba/1688). Ex-works US$1.5-5 per kit, MOQ 50-500 [estimate, unverified]. Lead time 7-15 days production plus 5-8 days air or 30-40 days sea [estimate].
- Originals: no wholesale terms found; De'Longhi, Jura and Philips reportedly restrict originals to authorised service partners [estimate]. Resale margin 25-35% unverified (DuoRoller 557 CZK gives about 4.7-6.6 EUR per brush).
- Landed cost, S8-type kit, 500 pcs (all estimate): ex-works 3.3 + freight 0.8-1.6 + duty about 0.1 + packaging 0.3 + returns reserve 0.6 = **5.1-5.9 EUR**.
- Fulfilment: pick/pack 1.5-2.5 EUR plus carrier 2-3.5 EUR = 4-5.5 EUR per order [estimate].
- Margin recalculation (judge): the "55-60%" after fulfilment holds only at 24 EUR ex-VAT. At 12-15 EUR ex-VAT (realistic kit price) it is about 25-35%.
- Gross margin base by group before fulfilment: compatible kits/singles 65%, originals 25%, coffee seals/brew groups 50%, descaler 20%. Blended after fulfilment 35-45% [estimate].
- Customs: bulk/pallet import avoids the per-parcel 3 EUR duty; normal duty rates and HS codes were not looked up. A 2 EUR handling fee is unconfirmed.
- **Supply risk: Medium.** Same factories as the Chinese marketplace sellers (no moat), 10+ new models a year (dead stock), IP takedowns, FX/freight 3-5 points.

## 6. Legal and regulatory (research summary, not legal advice)
EUR-Lex and e-Sbírka were unreachable; findings rest on snippets and general knowledge.
- Trade: goods trade is a free trade (volná živnost); repairs would be a separate trade and are not planned.
- Li-ion batteries (Reg. 2023/1542, Act 542/2020): importer is probably a "producer", so ECOBAT membership and ISOH2 reporting, about 300-1,000 EUR/year [estimate]; air-freight dangerous-goods surcharge 20-80 EUR per shipment [estimate]. Exclude batteries.
- WEEE for spare parts: unconfirmed; whole appliances would need registration.
- Descaler: CLP labelling, Czech SDS, poison-centre notification, possible biocide status; 300-1,500 EUR one-off [estimate, unconfirmed]. Exclude it.
- Brew groups/gaskets: food-contact declarations (Reg. 1935/2004) possibly needed.
- GPSR: EU responsible person required, about 150-600 EUR/year [estimate].
- Trademarks/design rights: "compatible with" wording only, no logos; Chinese "original" parts are likely counterfeit.
- Consumer law: 14-day withdrawal (hygiene exception may cover opened filters/pads), 24-month warranty, ČOI supervision.
- Reminders: opt-in under Act 480/2004 s.7 (soft opt-in for own customers' similar goods), fines up to CZK 10m; GDPR applies.
- VAT: VAT-payer s.r.o. recommended (21% import VAT recoverable, EORI needed); EU distance-selling threshold 10k EUR, then OSS.
- Right to repair: the Czech bill amending Act 634/1992 had its second reading on 24 June 2026 [sourced: psp.cz stenographic record, via search 2026-10-27]; final adoption and effective date unconfirmed. Slovakia reportedly effective 31 July 2026 [sourced: https://pravnenoviny.sk/pravo-na-opravu-tovaru/].
- Insurance: product liability about 300-800 EUR/year [estimate]. Use a 3PL rather than storing Li-ion at home.
- Start-up legal/compliance total 2,000-6,000 EUR [estimate] (scout said 1,000-2,500).
- Burden: Medium as briefed; Medium-to-Low if limited to non-electrical robot parts; High with whole appliances or a biocide permit.

## 8. Top 5 risks
1. Price compression and no moat (same factories as Kaufland sellers; Plus.Parts, Alza, Datart already in the niche).
2. Customer acquisition cost unmeasured (no CPC or search-volume data).
3. No Czech demand sizing; Spareka's French results do not transfer.
4. Brand/IP takedowns plus battery, descaler and brew-group compliance.
5. Dead stock from model churn against MOQs of 50-500 per model.

## 9. What would change the verdict
- A written quote for compatible kits at 3.5 EUR or less landed, MOQ 100 or fewer per model.
- Test CPA of 4 EUR or less across 3+ robot brands, or organic share above 40% of orders after 3 months.
- Evidence of 6+ orders/day demand (search volumes for the top 30 models, Heureka/Kaufland sales ranks).
- A distributor deal for originals at 35%+ margin.
- Repeat purchases above 30% within 6 months.
- Opposite: Spareka announcing CZ entry means stop.

## 10. 30-day validation plan (under 1,000 EUR)
1. Week 1: pull Czech search volume and CPC for 30 top robot models and parts queries (Keyword Planner); rank Heureka/Kaufland best-sellers.
2. Week 1: order samples from 3-5 suppliers (100-200 EUR); get written quotes with MOQ, landed cost, certificates; ask Czech/German distributors about originals.
3. Week 2: Shoptet trial or landing page with 10 SKUs, one model-finder page, 2 videos; "compatible with" wording only; accountant/lawyer check of the non-electrical compliance list (200-300 EUR).
4. Weeks 2-4: 300-500 EUR Google Ads; measure CPA, add-to-cart rate, reminder opt-ins; pre-sell a limited number of kits.
5. Week 4 go/no-go: go only if contribution before ads is 8+ EUR per order, CPA is 4 EUR or less, and there is a credible path to 170 orders/month.
