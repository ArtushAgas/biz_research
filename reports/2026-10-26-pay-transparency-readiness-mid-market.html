Pay-transparency readiness for mid-size Czech employers

A fixed-price service for employers with 50 to 250 staff that must prepare for the new Czech pay-transparency law: job architecture, pay bands, a pay-gap check and lawyer-reviewed templates. Assessed from the scout brief and three independent reports on competition, supply and the law.

Why, in five lines

  1. Demand exists but is thin and dated. The bill has only passed first reading. Near-term reporting duties hit employers with 150 to 249 staff, about 500 to 700 private employers in Prague and Central Bohemia (estimate). Employers with 50 to 99 staff have no reporting duty.
  2. The gap in price and segment is mostly not real. Pay-Fair already sells grading plus a parity report for 30,000 to 75,000 CZK a year. The ministry's Logib tool is free, and courses with sample documents cost about 143 EUR. The open gap is done-for-you implementation with legal sign-off.
  3. The templates run into the advocacy act. Drafting a client's pay regulation and information letters for a fee risks counting as unauthorised legal services (Act 85/1996 §2(3)). A lawyer has to be contracted separately, and that lawyer then owns the client.
  4. Hours and income are lower than the brief. A full package takes 25 to 45 hours, not 12, so capacity is 10 to 18 packages a year. Expect about 150 EUR a month in year 1 and about 1,250 EUR pre-tax in year 2 in the base case.
  5. The downside is small and testable. Risk is 5 to 14k EUR, and a 500 to 1,000 EUR validation can settle the key questions first.

What the law actually requires

Checked against the bill text (psp.cz, sněmovní tisk 300). Several of the scout's claims did not hold.

DateDutyApplies to
1 Jan 2027Written pay system (§109a). Tell applicants the minimum pay before signing, and no questions about past pay.All employers
1 Jan 2028Employees can ask for pay information, answered within 2 months.All employers
1 Jan 2028Gap report to the ministry by 30 April. 250+ staff annually, 150 to 249 every 3 years.150+ staff
1 Jan 2031First gap report.100 to 149 staff
From 2028Pay assessment if a 5%+ unjustified gap in a job group is not fixed in 6 months.Reporting employers (100+)
Corrections to the brief. The bill does not require pay ranges in job ads, only proof that the applicant got the minimum pay before signing. The bill has had its first reading (16 and 20 Oct 2026) and sits in committees until about 20 Nov. It has not had a second reading. Fines reach 1M CZK only for the most serious breaches; others are 200,000 to 400,000 CZK.

How to structure the service

  • Sell the method, data and process. The legal text (the §109a regulation, letters) comes from an advocate engaged separately, ideally by the client.
  • Pay the lawyer a fixed fee. No commission or referral fee either way.
  • Cap liability in the contract and get a professional indemnity quote at 5M CZK.
  • Act as a data processor with a processing agreement; do not reuse client data for benchmarks.
  • Do not claim "MPSV-certified", "official" or "guaranteed compliance".

Questions for a lawyer

  • Does drafting the §109a regulation or templates for a fee count as unauthorised legal services?
  • May you bundle the lawyer's fee into yours, or refer clients to them for a fee?
  • Which fine applies to a missing pay system, and from when?
  • Liability caps and the managing director's personal exposure.

Top risks and what would change the verdict

Top 5 risks

  • Legal structure. Without a lawyer the product is unsellable; with one, the lawyer owns the client.
  • Timing. A 1 Jan 2027 start needs the Chamber, Senate and President. Slippage of weeks to months is plausible, and many employers will wait for 2028.
  • Price anchoring. Pay-Fair, free Logib and cheap courses cap what buyers will pay for the commodity parts.
  • Credibility. A non-HR, non-lawyer owner against EY and PwC. The 5% test is statistically weak in small job groups.
  • Capacity and volume. 10 to 18 packages a year, in a one-time wave concentrated at 150 to 249 staff.

What would change my mind

  • An advocate confirms in writing a structure where the client hires the lawyer and you keep a defined scope.
  • At least 3 of 12 target employers have no plan and no vendor, and 2 agree to a paid 500 EUR diagnostic.
  • Leads cost under 80 EUR each, 5 or more from about 300 EUR of ads.
  • The bill passes second reading with the 1 Jan 2027 dates intact.
  • Pay-Fair or a C&B firm agrees to partner. Downgrade if the advocate says the drafting is unauthorised, or employers already bought elsewhere.
