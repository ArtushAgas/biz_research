# Decision report: Grounds maintenance for public landlords, SVJ and company sites (Prague and Central Bohemia)

*Date 2026-10-28. FX 24.3 CZK/EUR. Research summary, not legal advice. Written by the venture-judge from the idea brief and three independent reports (competition, supply, legal); condensed digests of those reports were passed to the judge.*

## 1. Header
- **Idea:** grounds maintenance (údržba zeleně: mowing, trimming, hedges, autumn clean-up, winter paths) with a 2-3 person crew. The brief mixes small public calls (ÚZSVM, NPÚ, army, police school, districts) with private SVJ and company sites.
- **Category:** local-physical (service).
- **Verdict as briefed: PASS. Score 56/100. Confidence: Low.** The screen said 57.
- **Reshaped variant (private-first, public calls only as reference-builders, no equipment bought before contracts exist): 60/100, borderline Investigate, Low confidence.** Only worth a 30-day zero-capex test.
- **Capital:** base capex about 435k CZK incl. VAT (17.9k EUR ex VAT) [estimate, from the supply report]. Peak cash need about 27-30k EUR including a 30-day payment lag. Low about 16k EUR, high about 35k EUR. All far below the 100k EUR cap.
- **Hours/week:** 15-20 in season, with ramp-up peaks above 20 in Mar-Jun; about 6-8 off-season [estimate].

## 2. Why in 5 lines
1. The business only works on private sites at 1.8-2.5 CZK/m2 ex VAT per cut. The model shows a loss at about 1.0 CZK/m2, where the public anchors sit. The 57 from the screen rested on a +3 public lens that has no verified award price.
2. The competition report has a 10x arithmetic error: 2 CZK/m2 x 15 cuts is 300,000 CZK/ha/year, not 30,000. So "20-25 ha needed" is wrong; the correct requirement is about 5-6 ha of recurring contracts at base assumptions (about 10-20 sites). Achievable, but only if productivity and price hold.
3. Base case: year 1 earns about 186k CZK (7.7k EUR) pre-tax and year 2 about 257k CZK (21k CZK/month), just under the 25k CZK/month target. Cumulative payback is around month 24. The low case never pays back.
4. Public channel: unit prices 0.8-1.2 CZK/m2, price wars of -33% to -79% against estimates, reference and headcount requirements, almost no Prague/Central Bohemia small calls (about 5 of 50 NEN hits), and no ÚOHS recourse for small-scale calls (VZMR). A reference-builder at best.
5. The owner has no grounds background and 20 h/week. Customer acquisition (SVJ managers already have vendors), weather, crew reliability and the Nov-Feb dead season land on the owner. The score is capped by price risk and part-time load, not by capital.

## 4. Concept and why Prague / why now
- **Concept:** a 2-3 person crew with a ride-on or zero-turn mower, brush cutters, hedge trimmers, trailer and van. Serves SVJ and company sites on annual fixed-price contracts, adds hedges, leaves and clean-up, and bids on small public calls to build references.
- **Why Prague:** the owner's cleaning-services network reaches the same property managers and facility buyers who buy grounds work. Density near Prague (e.g. Praha-východ) keeps travel low.
- **Why now:** no special catalyst found. Spring SVJ budget meetings are the selling window, so the test must run Nov-Feb to be ready for 2027.
- **Weakness:** no grounds-keeping background and no references.

## 7. Legal and regulatory
- **Burden: medium** (high if herbicide spraying becomes core; medium-high for public VZMR). Lawyer/accountant must verify regulated items.
- Garden services are a volná živnost (licences not assessed per profile). VZMR limit is 3,000,000 CZK for services since 3 Apr 2025 [zakonyprolidi 2016-134, valid to 31 Dec 2026]. §6 principles apply; qualification only if the call requires it (§79(2)(b), references of the last 3 years); §83 allows another person's qualification with a commitment to perform.
- **Herbicide:** a Level II certificate (15 h course plus exam, valid 5 years) is needed to apply for others [MZe FAQ]; the supply report's "Level I" is wrong for a business. Some products banned within 20 m of public parks (§52b(3)). **Plan: do not offer herbicide in year 1.**
- Green waste: the crew may be the producer (§5(2), Act 541/2020) unless the contract says otherwise; carrier registration (§§80-82) unverified. Trailers over 750 kg need B96/BE. Machinery Regulation 2023/1230 applies from 20 Jan 2027 to new equipment. Motor insurance for machines above 14 km/h unclear for a 10-13 km/h zero-turn.
- Minimum wage 22,400 CZK/month (134.40 CZK/h, also for DPP); DPP max 300 h/year per employer; income above 12,000 CZK/month triggers full contributions. Corporate tax 21% [estimate, verify]; dividend tax a further layer [estimate 15%, verify].
- **Recommended entity:** non-VAT s.r.o. for SVJ, non-VAT companies and public calls scoring price incl. VAT (a VAT payer must add 21%); the VAT-payer s.r.o. for VAT-registered corporate clients and equipment purchase (about 92k CZK input VAT recoverable). Never bid with both entities on one call (anti-splitting risk). Confidence medium.
- **Open questions for a lawyer or accountant:** (1) VAT trigger on a 2.5-3.0M CZK win; (2) reverse charge status of CPA 81.30; (3) references and splitting rules in VZMR; (4) DPP/DPČ structure for a seasonal crew; (5) waste-carrier registration; (6) public-space fees; (7) insurance minimums; (8) changes from 1 Jan 2027 including a ZZVZ amendment; (9) EET 2.0 (1 Jan 2027, in-person payments only, irrelevant for invoice-paid work).

## 8. First-year financial sketch (season 2027 as "year 1")
All figures pre-tax, ex VAT, FX 24.3.

**Judge reconciliations**
1. **10x error.** 2 CZK/m2 x 15 cuts = 300k CZK/ha/year (the Praha 10 full-service lots at about 32-38 CZK/m2/year confirm the order of magnitude). "20-25 ha needed" was wrong; model rebuilt.
2. **Supply model vs legal estimate.** Crew cost: supply uses market wages (82k CZK gross for three people x1.34, about 110k CZK/month employer cost); legal used the minimum wage (about 3,700 EUR/month for three). Market wages are realistic. Kit: legal's 4-9k EUR is basic kit without a ride-on, van or snow blower; supply's 17.9k EUR ex VAT is the full productive set; judge uses 8k EUR low and 17.9k EUR base.
3. **Optimistic supply margins.** Judge uses 1,300-1,900 m2/crew-hour and 900-1,150 CZK/crew-hour labour (travel, weather and idle time included), adds machine and van at 310 CZK/h, fixed costs 11.5k CZK/month, 3% bad debt and rework, and disposal 0.15 CZK/m2 on half the cuts.
4. **Sanity check.** Base gives about 3.4k CZK revenue per crew-hour, roughly 1.0-1.5M CZK revenue per employee-year; plausible, not proven.

## 9. Top 5 risks
1. **Price and productivity unmeasured.** Below about 1.5 CZK/m2 or about 1,200 m2/h the business loses money; private contract prices are unpublished and public anchors are low.
2. **Customer acquisition without references.** SVJ managers have vendors and decide at annual meetings; 10-20 signed sites are needed by June; Google Ads CPC for this niche unverified.
3. **Public channel is a trap for newcomers.** Price-only awards, -33% to -79% below estimates, reference and headcount rules, few Prague-area small calls, an incumbent on ÚZSVM, no ÚOHS route for VZMR, VAT trigger on a 2.6-3.0M CZK win.
4. **Part-time load, weather and seasonality.** 15-20 h/week at peak, crew reliability, rain days, breakdowns, a four-month dead winter, on-call snow work that conflicts with part-time ownership.
5. **Compliance and liability.** Herbicide certificate, waste duties, damage and injury insurance (unquoted), crew classification, two-s.r.o. split-bidding rules.

## 10. What would change my mind
- Three or more signed quotes or LOIs from SVJ or company sites at 1.8 CZK/m2 or more ex VAT, totalling at least 3 ha within about 20 km.
- Measured crew productivity of at least 1,500 m2 per crew-hour from a ride-along or trial on a real SVJ-style site.
- A Prague or Central Bohemia public award with a stated per-m2 price of 1.5 or more (e.g. the ÚZSVM result for N006/25/V00031802), or a framework holder offering a subcontract at 1.2 CZK/m2 net or more.
- A property manager agreeing to a pilot portfolio of 3 ha or more.
- A partner who already has crew and equipment (joint venture).
- A written insurance quote below 20k CZK/year and a confirmed carrier and herbicide position from the accountant.
- Any of these would move the reshaped variant to about 65 and Medium confidence. Quotes at or below 1.2 CZK/m2 would end the idea.

## 11. 30-day validation plan (under about 10k CZK, no equipment bought)
**Week 1, price discovery:** list about 40 SVJ and company sites within about 15 km of one cluster (e.g. Praha-východ) and measure areas on Mapy.cz or the cadastre; ask 5 competitors for quotes on 3 sample sites (mystery shopper); ask 3 property managers in the owner's cleaning network whether they would tender grounds work for 2027 and who the current vendor is.
**Week 2, demand test:** one-page site with an m2 quote form; Google Ads for 3-5k CZK to check CPC and lead cost; pitch 15 SVJ and companies through cleaning-network introductions; target 3 written LOIs.
**Week 3, public channel facts:** call the ÚZSVM regional workplace about N006/25/V00031802 and ask for the award notice (winner, value, bids, price basis incl./ex VAT, reference requirement), with an Act 106/1999 request as fallback; check Registr smluv for the framework contract; read the Praha 17 and KSÚS DNS terms and confirm late applications are allowed; ask 2-3 framework holders for subcontract rates.
**Week 4, cost and compliance:** ride-along or productivity benchmark from an existing gardening firm; insurance quote; accountant opinion on VAT reverse charge, waste, DPP structure and the VAT trigger; seasonal labour rates from 2 sources.
**Gate (day 30):** buy used equipment in Feb-Mar 2027 only if all hold: (1) at least 3 ha of signed LOIs at 1.8 CZK/m2 or more ex VAT; (2) measured productivity of at least 1,500 m2 per crew-hour; (3) an insurance quote below 25k CZK/year. Otherwise park the idea.
