# Honey spoons (and agave): follow-up deep dive

Date 2026-11-21. Owner-requested follow-up to `reports/2026-10-27-honey-spoons-single-serve-cz.md` (44/100, variant 51/100, Pass). Built from three independent research reports (customers/competition, supply/economics, legal). FX about 24.3 CZK/EUR [estimate]. Research summary, not legal advice. This is an analysis, not a new formal judge score.

## Bottom line
- The idea is cheap to test (EUR 300-500) and has a real angle: a spoon of honey sells as a gift at 3-10x the price of a commodity portion. It is not a proven business, and on current numbers it reaches roughly a quarter to half of the EUR 1,000/month target in year one, unless one larger channel lands.
- Best framing: a premium gift product with a named Czech apiary story, sold B2B-first (corporate gifts, weddings/events, souvenir shops), not a mass e-shop product.
- Agave: weak. No single-serve agave product found anywhere, no cost advantage, and a honey + agave mix cannot be sold as honey. Treat it as a later add-on, not part of the first product.

## 2. Competition
- No Czech seller of pre-filled honey spoons found. Substitutes: Hellma/Langnese/Breitsamer sticks (EUR 0.19-0.28), empty honey spoons (EUR 3.5-13), jar gift sets, Manuka sachets (EUR 0.85-1.00 each).
- US proof of a gift niche: Some Honey spoons USD 0.60-0.90 each; Uncommon Goods 12 spoons USD 25; Crate & Barrel 12-spoon set USD 29.95 (about USD 2-2.5 per spoon) [sourced, 2026-11-21].
- Czech beekeepers and gift brands (Medokom, Včelí banka, Pleva, Elisa) could add spoons quickly once demand shows. Design and price are not moats; origin story and B2B customisation are.

## 3. Economics
Cost per finished spoon (10 g) [estimates; no public co-packer quote exists]:
| Route | 2,000 | 10,000 | 50,000 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Czech/EU co-packer, wooden spoon | EUR 0.45 | 0.35 | 0.28 |
| Asia white label | 0.62 | 0.40 | 0.29 |
| Own filling machine (machine ~EUR 7k + premises ~3.6k/yr) | 0.43 variable | 0.29 | 0.21 |
| Hand-fill with sealed film | 0.48 | 0.38 | n/a |

- Honey itself is only EUR 0.04-0.08 per spoon (private-label jar honey ~CZK 191/kg ex-VAT [sourced, Beedol]; Polish barrel ~EUR 3.4-4.0/kg [estimate]). Cost is in the spoon, film, filling, labels and shipping, not in the honey.
- Agave organic ~EUR 4.9/kg [sourced, German retail], about EUR 0.05 per spoon: no saving versus honey. Honey-only and honey+agave economics are within about EUR 0.03 per spoon.
- Break-even for EUR 1,000/month profit:
  - B2C base (CZK 20 per spoon incl. 12% VAT, 3 ten-packs per order, after acquisition cost): contribution about EUR 0.09 per spoon, so about 13,800 spoons a month.
  - B2B at EUR 0.50 per spoon: about 11,400 a month (8,300 with own machine). At EUR 0.65: about 4,000 a month.
  - Gift/corporate box at a higher ticket (for example 10 spoons for CZK 200-250) is the only route near 4,000 a month.
- Machines seen: fill+seal spoon machines USD 5.9-8.7k, small sealer about USD 1,019, straw filler about USD 25k [sourced, listings]. Own filling only makes sense above about 50,000 units a year, and brings food-premises hygiene duties.

Gap: the realistic volume (1,000-4,200 spoons a month) is below the 4,000-11,400 needed. Expect EUR 100-500/month at the end of year one in a good case, and a loss in a weak one.

## 4. Legal (research summary)
- **Plastic spoon is banned.** Act 243/2022 §6, Part A item 2 (cutlery incl. spoons); fines to CZK 5M; enforced by the hygiene station (KHS), which can order withdrawal. A wooden or paper spoon is probably outside, but §3 covers items "wholly or partly of plastic", so coating or plastic film wrap, and PLA "compostable" spoons, need a lawyer's view.
- **Honey law changed on 14.6.2026.** Decree 76/2003 is repealed and replaced by Decree 490/2025; Directive 2024/1438 origin labelling applies (multi-origin blends list countries with percentages; under 30 g packs may use two-letter country codes). Lot marking and best-before apply.
- **Honey cannot be mixed with other foods** (490/2025 §16(5)). A honey + agave product cannot be called honey; it needs another name and full ingredient list. The same question hits flavoured honey (ginger, cinnamon). A lawyer must confirm the name.
- **Small-pack label exemption** (under 10 cm² largest surface) probably does not apply to a stick/sachet or spoon pack; plan for full labels.
- Registration: free notification before starting (SVS for animal products, SZPI otherwise; which one applies to honey packing is unconfirmed); HACCP plan; food-contact declaration for the spoon and film; with a co-packer you still carry labelling and recall liability as the brand owner.
- Packaging EPR (Act 477/2001, EKO-KOM) and EU PPWR from 12.8.2026 apply; whether single-use plastic portions are restricted from 2030 (Annex V) for retail honey is unconfirmed. VAT 12% on honey and likely agave (chapters 04, 17). Recommended entity: the VAT-payer s.r.o.
- Health claims ("soothes sore throat") are not allowed. A voluntary "not for infants under 12 months" note is common practice.
- Burden: Medium for honey-only with a wooden/paper spoon; Medium-High for a honey + agave product; prohibited for plastic spoons.

## 5. Top risks
1. Volume: realistic orders are below the break-even; the target needs one larger channel.
2. Low ticket: acquisition cost wipes out B2C margin (about EUR 0.09 per spoon).
3. No confirmed co-packer or quote (MOQ conflicts, 1,000 vs 20,000 units).
4. Legal classification: wooden spoon with film, honey + agave naming, flavoured honey, which authority registers packing.
5. Copycats: beekeepers and gift brands can add spoons quickly.
6. Product quality: crystallisation, 3-10% seal leakage, honey adulteration, sticky breakage.
7. Seasonality: about half the B2C volume lands in Q4; the 2026 corporate window is nearly closed.

## 6. What would make it work
- A quote of CZK 8 or less per finished wooden spoon at an MOQ of 2,000-5,000.
- Two or more corporate, event or café buyers committing to 500+ spoons a month at EUR 0.45+.
- A lawyer/MŽP view that wood + film is acceptable and a clear name for any non-honey blend.
- Search volume for "medová lžička" / "medové tyčinky" that shows real demand.

## 7. 30-day test (EUR 300-500)
- **Week 1:** write to the Ministry of Environment and ask a lawyer the open questions (wooden spoon and film, honey + agave name, SVS vs SZPI); ask Medokom/Medokomerc, Beedol, Miody Polskie and Monodosis de Aceite (ES) for co-packed quotes with MOQ, shelf life and food-contact papers; pull Keyword Planner and Sklik for Czech and German terms.
- **Weeks 1-3:** 20 emails to HR and corporate-gift agencies for Q1-Q2 2027 events and Christmas 2027 briefs; 10 hand-delivered samples to tea shops and Prague souvenir shops; landing page with mock-ups and a 14-day Google Ads test (waitlist or pre-order).
- **Weeks 2-4:** 5 sample sets for wedding planners via Instagram.
- **Day-30 gate:** proceed only if quotes meet the cost target and at least 2 B2B commitments (500+ spoons a month at EUR 0.45+) or 10 genuine pre-orders appear. Otherwise stop with under EUR 500 spent.
