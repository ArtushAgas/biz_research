## 2. Why in 5 lines

1. Demand exists but is thin and dated. The bill has only passed first reading; the near-term reporting pool is employers with 150-249 staff (about 500-700 private employers in Prague and Central Bohemia, judge's estimate). The 50-99 band has no reporting duty.
2. The "gap in price and segment" is mostly not real. Pay-Fair already sells grading plus an internal parity report for CZK 30-75k a year (EUR 1.2-3.1k), Logib is free, and sample-document courses cost EUR 143. The open gap is done-for-you implementation with legal sign-off.
3. The "templates" product runs into Act 85/1996 §2(3) (unauthorised legal services). The §109a regulation and information letters are the documents most likely to be caught. A lawyer must be contracted separately, and that lawyer then controls the client.
4. The scout's 12 delivery hours is realistic only for a templated starter; a full package is 25-45 hours, so capacity is ~10-18 packages a year. The scout's EUR 5-8k/month is not credible. Base gives about EUR 150/month in year 1 and about EUR 1,250 pre-tax in year 2.
5. The downside is small (EUR 5-14k), but the upside is a one-time compliance wave in a field where the owner has no credentials and no HR/law network. A EUR 500-1,000 validation can settle it first.

## 4. Concept and why Prague / why now

- **Concept.** Fixed-price service for 50-250 staff employers: job architecture and pay bands, gender pay-gap analysis, templates, lawyer review. Target EUR 2.5-6k core package plus workshops and annual refresh.
- **Corrected why-now.** EU Directive 2023/970 deadline 7 Jun 2026 passed (only SK, IT, LT, MT transposed, Forvis Mazars 15 Sep 2026, secondary). Czech bill sněmovní tisk 300: first reading 16 and 20 Oct 2026, sent to committees. Slovakia Act 76/2026 in force since 7 Jun 2026. CZ unadjusted gender pay gap ~18.5% vs EU 11.1%.
- **Why Prague.** Largest employer pool (~3,700 employers with 50-249 staff in Prague + Central Bohemia in 2020, ~4,000 in 2023 [estimate]), but also where Pay-Fair, Accace and EY sit.
- **Natural beachhead.** The owner's cleaning and logistics network: shift-based, low-wage, multi-role employers of 50-250 staff with no HR department. An access advantage, not a credential.

## 7. Legal and regulatory (research summary, not legal advice)

Verified against the bill (psp.cz tisk 300):

| Date | Duty | Applies to |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Jan 2027 | §109a written pay system | All employers |
| 1 Jan 2027 | §30: tell applicants minimum pay before signing; no pay-history questions | All employers |
| 1 Jan 2028 | Employee information rights, answered within 2 months | All employers |
| 1 Jan 2028 | Reporting by 30 April for the prior year | 250+ annually; 150-249 every 3 years |
| 1 Jan 2031 | First reporting | 100-149 staff |
| 1 Jan 2028 onward | Pay assessment | Only reporting employers (100+), if an unjustified gap of 5%+ in a job group is not fixed in 6 months |

Fines up to CZK 1M for the most serious breaches (no pay system, no report, no assessment), CZK 200-400k for others.

**Burden: MEDIUM, conditional.** Becomes HIGH if the business drafts legal documents without advocate sign-off.

**Recommended structure** [confirm with a Czech Bar Association advocate]: advocate signs off templates as a separate engagement, ideally contracted directly with the client; no commission or referral fee; liability cap in the contract; professional indemnity quote at CZK 5M.

**Other.** Entity: VAT-payer s.r.o. (B2B, 21% VAT, reverse charge for EU customers, corporate tax 21%, dividend withholding 15%); non-VAT s.r.o. only as a pilot. GDPR: owner is a processor, needs a DPA, no cross-client benchmark reuse, EU-hosted tools. Marketing: do not claim "MPSV-certified", "official" or "guaranteed compliance". Licence type: volná živnost "Poradenská a konzultační činnost" (cost ~1,000 CZK).

**Open questions for the lawyer.** 1) Does drafting §109a regulations or templates for a fee count as "sepisování listin"? 2) May the consultant bundle the lawyer's fee or refer for a fee? 3) Which fine applies to a missing §109a system, and from when? 4) Liability caps and the managing director's personal exposure.

## 9. Top 5 risks

1. **Legal-structure risk.** Core deliverables are "sepisování listin". Without a lawyer the product is unsellable; with one, the lawyer owns the client and the owner has no commission protection. The Haiku-run legal report calls it a gray zone; needs a Bar-registered advocate's view.
2. **Timing risk.** Bill is through first reading only. The 30-day committee window ends ~19-20 Nov, then second/third readings, Senate (~30 days) and President (~15 days). A 1 Jan 2027 start is tight; employers may wait for 2028 duties.
3. **Price and anchoring risk.** Pay-Fair at EUR 1.2-3.1k a year, free Logib, EUR 143 courses. EUR 2.5k+ must be justified by implementation and legal sign-off alone.
4. **Credibility risk.** Non-HR, non-lawyer owner against EY and PwC. The 5% test per job group is statistically weak at 50-250 staff; mistakes could create liability for client fines up to CZK 1M.
5. **Volume and capacity risk.** 25-45 hours per package, capacity 10-18 packages a year, one-time wave concentrated at 150-249 staff.

## 10. What would change my mind

**Upgrade to Investigate (60+) if all of these happen:** an advocate confirms in writing a structure where the client contracts the lawyer directly and the owner keeps a defined scope; at least 3 of 12 target employers say they have no plan and no vendor, and at least 2 agree to a paid diagnostic (EUR 500-800) or paid pilot; Google Ads/landing-page leads cost under EUR 80 each (at least 5 leads from ~EUR 300); the bill passes second reading before year end with 1 Jan 2027 dates intact, or a firm replacement date; a C&B partner or Pay-Fair agrees to a reseller/implementation arrangement.

**Downgrade to a hard no if:** the advocate says drafting §109a documents makes the consultant an unauthorised provider; interviews show employers already bought Pay-Fair, Accace or an EY audit, or "the accountant handles it"; delivery time stays above 30 hours after the pilot; the bill stalls or is watered down (check psp.cz after 20 Nov).

## 12. Data gaps and sources

**Spot-checked by the judge on 26 Oct 2026.**
- Bill status: psp.cz historie.sqw?o=10&t=300 confirms submitted 8 Sep 2026; first reading at the 34th session on 16 and 20 Oct; sent to committees (resolution 343) with a 30-day deadline; guarantor Social Policy Committee has not yet discussed it; further proceedings from 20 Nov 2026; amendment 1965 (Richterová) concerns unemployment benefits. **The legal report is right; the competition report's "second reading" is wrong.**
- Pay-Fair prices confirmed on the live page (S/M/L tables, per-year, ex VAT, CZK 1,750/h consulting; extra user access CZK 1,500 per 3 months or CZK 2,000 per year; the S/M boundary at exactly 150 staff is unclear).
- Reporting dates: web results agree with the legal report's reading of the bill (150+ from 2028, 100-149 from 2031); one 3 Sep 2026 secondary source shows a 100+ threshold, the source conflict the competition report mentioned.

**Reconciliations.**
- (a) Delivery hours 12 vs 25-45: supply is right for the full package (~30 base); 12 is plausible only for a templated starter (judge uses 13).
- (b) "Gap in price and segment": competition is right; the only gap is done-for-you implementation with a lawyer at EUR 2.5k+, a service-layer gap.
- (c) Bill status: first reading and committee stage, not second reading; the "30 days" is the committee deadline.
- (d) Job-ad claim: the legal report is right; §30(3) requires proving the applicant received the minimum pay before signing, which an ad satisfies but is not the only way. A pay-range-in-ads template is a minor deliverable and cannot carry the product. The recruiting hook is nearly commodity.
- (e) Thresholds: per the bill, 250+ annually from 2028, 150-249 every three years from 2028, 100-149 every three years from 2031, 50-99 no reporting duty. Directive thresholds could not be verified (EUR-Lex unreachable). Near-term reporting pool is the 150-249 band, ~500-700 private employers in Prague + Central Bohemia, derived from 2020 AMSP/ČSÚ band counts (low reliability; includes public sector and non-profits) [estimate].
- (f) Unauthorised legal services: Act 85/1996 §2(3) bars offering or brokering legal services through an unauthorised person. The owner can sell the method, data and process but not the legal text, which must be a separate advocate engagement paid by the client. From a Haiku-run summary; needs an advocate's view.

**Remaining gaps.** No Czech project prices from Big-4, Accace, ČSOB or boutiques; whether MPSV/EY audits are free to employers; the fine for a missing §109a system and the draft decrees; current 150-249 employer counts by region and private sector; Pay-Fair's financials and whether Accace runs on it; a professional indemnity quote; willingness to pay and acquisition cost; Bar fee-sharing rules; C&B day rates; Directive text; status after 26 Oct 2026.
