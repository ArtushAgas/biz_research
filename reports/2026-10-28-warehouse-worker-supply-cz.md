# Research report: supplying warehouse workers (Amazon and others) in Czechia

*Date 2026-10-28. Owner idea (hearsay): "providing workers to an Amazon warehouse, probably as a subcontractor". Three independent reports (market and competition, legal, economics and worker sourcing) synthesised by the orchestrator, with no separate judge, scored with the scoring-rubric. FX about 24.3 CZK/EUR. Research summary, not legal advice.*

## 1. Header
- **Idea:** warehouse-worker-supply-cz, supplying agency and temporary warehouse and logistics workers (pickers, packers, loaders, sorters, forklift operators) to Amazon and other fulfilment and logistics sites.
- **Verdict: PASS. Score 32/100. Confidence: Low-Medium** (the bill rate is derived, not observed).
- **The owner's claim ("subcontracting workers to Amazon is a great idea") is not supported.** An ordinary s.r.o. cannot lawfully supply workers who work under a client's direction without an agency permit, and Amazon's Czech agency channel is already served by large agencies.
- **Capital:** at 50 workers about 99k EUR (deposit 41k + working capital 42k + buffer 15k), i.e. the whole cap, for roughly the 25k CZK/month target after paying a required responsible representative.
- **Owner hours:** about 25-35 h/week at 50 workers, 24/7 shift pressure; not feasible at 100 workers.

## 2. Why in 5 lines
1. **Legal gate:** supplying workers under client direction is lawful only through a licensed agency (MPSV permit, fee 25,000 CZK, deposit 1,000,000 CZK, a responsible representative employed 20 h/week with 2-5 years of mediation practice). The unlicensed "subcontractor" pass-through is disguised placement: fines up to 10M CZK for a company, a ban up to 2 years, and client liability.
2. **Thin economics:** estimated bill rate about 325 CZK/h at a 205 CZK/h wage gives a net margin of about 8 / 18 / 30 CZK per billed hour (2.5 / 5.5 / 9%) after leave accrual, onboarding and no-shows. About 50 continuously placed workers are needed for 25k CZK/month once a representative is paid.
3. **Capital and cash:** deposit plus working capital (21-45k CZK per worker depending on pay frequency and client terms) hits the 100k EUR cap at about 50 workers; weekly pay or 60-day client terms break it.
4. **Market:** large (IBISWorld about 2.1bn EUR) but crowded and price-driven (1,631 licensed agencies in July 2024); Amazon's Dobrovíz and Kojetín labour is supplied by Adecco and Randstad at Amazon-set wages (225-247 CZK/h); robotics and a loosening labour market flatten demand for unskilled picking.
5. **Entry is possible but not cheap or small:** about 2 of 3 licence applications succeed and small agencies win public frameworks, but a newcomer without licence, references or capital has no room against incumbents.

## 4. Amazon and the market
- **Amazon in Czechia:** Dobrovíz (fulfilment, opened 2015, planned 2,000 roles), Kojetín (robotic, 2023, up to 2,000 jobs), Chomutov (printing). Seasonal staff 3,600+ in autumn 2024 [sourced: ceskenoviny.cz 2574834, 2024-09-27]. Current headcount unknown.
- **Who supplies agency labour:** Adecco (Dobrovíz ad 225-247 CZK/h, 247 from 2026-10-01 [sourced: pracomat.cz, 2026-07-25]) and Randstad HR Solutions (ran Kojetín recruitment). Pay matches Amazon's own published scale (231 CZK/h in 2024), so agencies work to Amazon-set pay. **No public evidence of tier-2 suppliers or a small-supplier programme; not verified.** At best a small supplier would be overflow behind Adecco or Randstad in peak months [estimate].
- **Agency market:** about 2.1bn EUR (IBISWorld, 1.0% CAGR 2021-26, no company above 5% share); 2,167 agencies (Dec 2023) fell to 1,631 (Jul 2024) after the 2024 deposit rise; 982 hold the leasing permit; applications 2024-2025: 858 filed, 569 granted, 161 refused [sourced: MPSV replies]. Players: Manpower (largest by self-claim), Adecco, Randstad, regional agencies (AMBOSELI, Netmarks, Lepší práce, Agency Plus), new entrant LIDEX AG (2024).
- **Warehouse wages (gross CZK/h, Prague and Central Bohemia, 2026):** 170-250, median about 195-205; Amazon level 225-247; Rohlik direct hire 200 + 50 bonus; minimum wage 134.40 CZK/h. **Bill rates charged to clients: no public rate card found.** Anchors: a 2014 public Randstad agreement priced at gross wage x 1.515; a Slovak public contract at labour cost x 1.14; agency commission 20-30 CZK/h (unverified snippets).
- **Demand signals:** Prague unemployment 3.93% (Sep 2026, 1.66 applicants per vacancy), Central Bohemia 4.2% (2.9 per vacancy), national 4.8% (looser than a year ago); 935k foreign workers (22% of employees); e-commerce 206bn CZK in 2025 (+6%).
- **Public frameworks (TED, 2026):** DPP Prague tram repairs (price-only, 350-380M CZK ceiling, SME winner Netmarks), EG.D 372M CZK (lowest price, e-auction, Manpower), Pražské služby (lowest margin, SME AMBOSELI), CzechInvest 121.8M CZK, ČEZ DNS. Buyers are state and city companies, not warehouses. SMEs won 3 of 4 awarded frameworks, so size is not a bar, but a licence, references and funding for a 15M CZK-per-quarter payroll are. Classification: subcontract only / price benchmark for a newcomer; NEN not verified.

## 5. Legal and regulatory (summary)
- **What is lawful:** agency employment (temporary assignment) only with a type-b MPSV permit (Act 435/2004 §14); a service-contract or "subcontract" wrapper where the client controls shifts, equipment or supervision is disguised placement (§5 g). A genuine managed service with the s.r.o. as the real employer and director of the work is lawful in principle but the boundary is blurred and needs real operations. Workers on IČO in a warehouse are illegal dependent work.
- **Penalties:** disguised placement up to 10M CZK for a company and 5M CZK for a natural person plus a ban up to 2 years; placement without licence up to 2M CZK; unreported foreign worker up to 3M CZK; the user (client) is also liable since 2 Aug 2021.
- **The permit:** MPSV; fee 25,000 CZK (15,000 repeat); deposit 1,000,000 CZK (about 41k EUR, locked until 60 days after the licence ends; the 500,000 CZK figure in some sources is outdated); responsible representative employed at least 20 h/week with a degree plus 2 years or maturita plus 5 years of mediation practice in the last 10 years (the owner has none, so a paid representative is likely); debt-free status checked every 6 months; premises marked; lead time about 3-6 months [estimate].
- **Agency work rules (Labour Code §307a-§309):** written agency-user agreement, comparable pay with the user's own staff, maximum 12 consecutive months with the same user, no DPP, assignment via employment contract or DPČ.
- **Foreign workers:** agencies may not place foreigners in jobs below maturita level except listed occupations (NV 64/2009), so picker and packer roles look closed to foreigners without free labour-market access; Ukrainians with temporary protection have free access (protection extended per secondary sources); a 2026 "repeat-licence only" rule appears in one blog and is unverified.
- **VAT:** labour supply is a taxable supply at 21%; the domestic reverse charge does not apply to warehouse labour; a non-VAT s.r.o. cannot invoice VAT, so a licensed agency should sit in the VAT-payer s.r.o.
- **Pending:** a platform/dependent-work bill (submitted 8 Sep 2026, in committee 16 Oct 2026, proposed from 1 Dec 2026) would add joint liability down the supply chain; KOBRA 26 targets agencies and foreign labour.
- **Burden:** unlicensed pass-through Prohibitive; licensed agency High; genuine managed service Medium.

## 6. Unit economics and cash (economics report, estimates)
- **Per billed hour at a 205 CZK/h wage:** wage 205 + employer levies 69.3 (33.8%) + accident insurance 1 + leave and sick accrual 24.8 = loaded payroll 300; recruiting and onboarding 4, no-shows 1.5, payroll admin 1, insurance 0.5 gives variable cost about 307.
- **Margin per billed hour:** low 8 CZK (bill 315), base 18 (bill 325), high 30 (bill 337); per worker-month 1.1k / 2.5k / 4.2k CZK. The legal report's 24% coefficient on wage cost gives a gross spread of about 7,200-10,000 CZK per worker-month, before leave accrual, turnover and overhead, which is consistent with a much smaller net margin.
- **Workers needed for 25,000 CZK/month profit:** without a representative 40 / 18 / 11 (low / base / high); with a paid representative (about 100k CZK/month fixed [estimate]) 113 / 50 / 30. Break-even about 8 workers without a representative, about 40 with one.
- **Working capital:** monthly pay and 30-day terms about 20.7k CZK per worker (50 workers 42.5k EUR, 100 workers 85k EUR); weekly pay about 44.7k CZK per worker; 60-day terms about 63.7k CZK per worker. Total launch cash at 50 workers, best case: working capital 42.5k + deposit 41.1k + 3 months of fixed costs about 12k + setup 3k = about 99k EUR. 100 workers is about 141k EUR; weekly pay at 50 workers about 148k EUR. Factoring at 1-2% a month would erase 3-7 CZK/h of the margin.
- **Q4 peak:** headcount can double or triple in November-December, doubling cash needs for 3-4 months, then falling in Q1 while fixed costs continue.
- **Owner time:** 20 workers 10-15 h/week; 50 workers 25-35; 100 workers not feasible alone.

## 7. Top 5 risks
1. **Disguised placement and legal exposure** (fines up to 10M CZK, 2-year ban, client liability, pending joint-liability bill).
2. **Margin squeeze:** net 2.5-9% of the bill rate; wages rise faster than cost-plus contracts adjust (minimum wage +7.7% in 2026).
3. **Cash and client concentration:** one client failing to pay for 1-2 months wipes 6-12 months of profit; the deposit locks 41k EUR.
4. **Amazon-specific:** pay and vendor management set by Amazon, Adecco and Randstad hold the channel, robotics reduce temp roles.
5. **Operations:** no-shows, about 100% annual turnover [estimate], 24/7 shifts versus a 20 h/week owner.

## 8. What would change my mind
- Three real bill-rate quotes at 380+ CZK/h for a skilled niche (forklift, night, German-speaking) or a verified net margin of 40+ CZK/h.
- A licensed agency that offers a revenue-share partnership with no deposit and no payroll risk for the owner.
- Confirmation that a placement-only (licence type a) route needs no deposit and is lawful for the owner's fee structure.
- Two or three logistics contacts in the owner's network committing to volume at 30-day payment terms outside Amazon.
- A representative on a statutory-body or low-cost basis.
Any of these moves the reshaped variant to about 50 and Medium confidence.

## 9. 30-day validation plan (under about 10k CZK, no deposit paid)
1. **Week 1, lawyer (about 2-3k CZK):** confirm the lawful structure options, whether a type-a placement-only licence (fee 5,000 CZK) avoids the deposit, the representative route, and the status of the platform bill and foreigner placement limits.
2. **Week 1-2, price discovery:** request quotes as a prospective client from 3 licensed agencies for 10 warehouse pickers (bill rate, markup, terms), and call 2-3 logistics contacts in the owner's network about demand, wage levels and payment terms.
3. **Week 2-3, partner test:** ask 2 regional agencies (not Adecco, Randstad or Manpower) about a revenue-share or referral arrangement and what they pay per placed worker.
4. **Week 3-4, niche check:** price forklift (W2), night-shift and German-speaking picker demand with 2-3 3PLs.
**Gate:** continue only if a quote or partner term shows a net margin of at least 40 CZK per billed hour or a referral fee that pays at least 25,000 CZK/month at 10-15 placements per month with no deposit and no payroll risk. Otherwise park the idea.
