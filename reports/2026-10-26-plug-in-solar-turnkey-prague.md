## 2. Why in 5 lines

1. The margin is not there. Realistic base gross on the kit is ~65 EUR (~14%) at ~13,500 CZK. Lidl and Kaufland anchor the bare kit at 8-10k CZK.
2. The paperwork selling point is weaker than the scout assumed. The application is a simple contract form, not a notification (vyhláška 16/2016 §16), and it disappears if the simplified regime passes. Real pain points are zero-export hardware, meter swap and SVJ/landlord consent.
3. The market is unproven: no CZ unit count, one seller at ~20 kits/month in 2023, and the only close prior attempt (Be4You on Donio, July 2024) raised 0 of 300k CZK.
4. Regulatory burden is HIGH as briefed: importer/distributor duties under GPSR, WEEE, product liability, and an unresolved "860 Wp vs 800 W" question. Stock bought now sits through winter and can strand if the law changes.
5. A no-stock variant fits the owner's skills (Google Ads, shops, building network) and costs under 10k EUR, but earns little per customer: a test, not yet a business.

## 4. Concept and why Prague / why now

- **Concept.** Import 860 Wp / 800 W sets from DE/PL, sell through a niche e-shop with bracket, paperwork done for the customer and optional installer referral.
- **Why now.** Germany had ~1.53M registered units at end of Sept 2026, 327,044 added Jan-Sept and 28,647 in September (-32% vs August) [sourced: https://kleineskraftwerk.de/blogs/magazin/balkonkraftwerke-september-2026-zahlen, Oct 2026; re-checked by the judge].
- **Why it does not carry over.** The German boom came from near-zero bureaucracy, not services. The September drop also shows seasonality and a plateau. Czechia has no equivalent regime today.
- **Czech reform.** Amendment 1054 (Jurečka): free e-notification for inverter up to 800 VA, panels up to 2 kWp, no battery. Reportedly not adopted by the Chamber on 10 July 2026 (date unconfirmed by some sources); the Senate reportedly returned the whole building-law novela on 20 August. No October 2026 status found. Check psp.cz (tisk 67) first.
- **Reform paradox.** If it passes, paperwork revenue vanishes but the market grows. If not, paperwork stays but the market stays small.
- **Why Prague.** Dense flats (~3M Czechs live in apartment buildings), PREdistribuce is the main distributor, and the owner's building/cleaning network reaches SVJ and property managers.

## 7. Legal and regulatory

### Reconciling the scout's description of the law
The judge adopts the legal report (primary law cited); the scout's claims were unsourced.
- Application route: vyhláška 16/2016 §16 + Annex 4 provide "zjednodušené připojení" for microsources up to 10.8 kW, a contract application with reserved capacity 0, **not** a notification. The §28(5) notification route (deemed consent after 30 days) exists, but ČEZ's page for it asks for revision report, protection-settings protocol and more, so it is poorly suited to plug-in kits.
- "Often refused": unsourced, dropped.
- Revision report and impedance measurement are required only if total installed output at the supply point is above 800 W.
- ČEZ requiring a revision report: unverified; may stem from reading 860 Wp as above 800 W.
- PRE (Prague's main distributor): no impedance measurement and no A1 document up to 800 W with zero export, but technical export prevention and a load-profile meter are required (page age unknown). EG.D has a dedicated 800 W no-export category.
- **Product consequence:** the 860 Wp / 800 VA spec is a compliance risk. Spec the kit as 2x400 Wp and get PRE's written confirmation before buying stock.
- **Service consequence:** the paperwork is a short form customers can do themselves. The paid service is mostly consent, hardware compatibility and hand-holding.

### Checklist
- **Trade licences.** Retail (volná item 48), brokerage (47), consultancy (60, lawyer to confirm for "paperwork + plná moc"): free trades, 500-1,000 CZK each. Installing is a řemeslná trade with qualification, so refer it out. Check both s.r.o. on rzp.cz first (free).
- **Importer/distributor role.** GPSR (since 13.12.2024): distributor, or importer if the manufacturer is outside the EU. CE file, labelling, 10-year technical file: major cost drivers.
- **WEEE.** MŽP registration + collective system, cost unknown.
- **Product liability.** New EU directive applies to products placed after 9 Dec 2026. Insurance ~1,000-3,000 EUR/yr combined [estimate].
- **Consumer law.** 14-day withdrawal, 24-month warranty; "withdrawal button" proposed from 1.1.2027.
- **GDPR.** Handling customer name, EAN, address makes the company controller or processor.
- **RfG Type A threshold.** An 800 W inverter sits exactly at the threshold; ask the distributor whether an A1 document is needed.
- **Unauthorized feed-in penalty** (~1,700 CZK/kWh): unverified, not used.
- **Outdated reference.** Vyhláška 50/1978 was repealed 1.7.2022 (Act 250/2021).
- **VAT.** 21% appears to apply to kits and installation; a reduced 12% rate is unverified, accountant to confirm.
- **Burden: HIGH as briefed**; MEDIUM for pure retail, which has the thinnest margin.

### Recommended entity
The existing **VAT-payer s.r.o.**: reclaims input VAT, holds a VAT ID for intra-EU purchases, absorbs the thresholds. The non-VAT s.r.o. would lose input VAT and trip the 2M CZK turnover and ~326,000 CZK acquisition limits. Liability ring-fencing in a new s.r.o. is a lawyer question.

### Open questions for a lawyer (~1 hour)
1. Is an 860 Wp / 800 VA kit "above 800 W"? 2. Is an A1 document needed for an 800 W inverter? 3. Does a layperson plugging in satisfy Energy Act §28(7)(b)? 4. Does a seller who mounts a bracket need an electro licence? 5. Licence, plná moc and GDPR roles for the paperwork service. 6. Importer/distributor role and WEEE. 7. VAT rate and acquisition threshold. 8. Liability ring-fencing, insurance, SVJ consent under Act 67/2013.

## 9. Top 5 risks
1. **Margin and price war.** Lidl/Kaufland move prices; the compliant tier is already 12.7k; 14% gross absorbs almost no surprise.
2. **Regulatory swing.** Simplified regime passes: paperwork revenue vanishes and stock strands. It fails: market stays small.
3. **Compliance and liability.** Importer duties, the 860 Wp threshold ambiguity, zero-export hardware that does not work with the kit, fire or injury claims aimed at the seller.
4. **Demand unproven.** No CZ volumes; the only comparable service launch got zero pledges.
5. **Third-party dependency.** Installers, revizní technik, distributors and SVJs control the customer's outcome; a failed connection becomes the owner's reputational problem.

## 10. What would change my mind
- A written quote of ≤230 EUR landed for a 2x400 Wp kit at 40 pcs (or dropship ~260), with CE DoC and grid-code certificate (lifts base gross above 120 EUR).
- Written PRE (and ČEZ/EG.D) confirmation that the chosen kit plus a named export-limiting device connects with no revision and no A1 document.
- At least 8 paid, refundable deposits for a 2,500 CZK+ pack within the test at ≤30 EUR per lead.
- An installer/revizní pool paying 75 EUR+ per referral or subcontracting at a fixed price.
- Legislative news: simplified regime passed, pivot to accessories and lead-gen at scale; rejected for good, pursue only the compliance-pack niche.
- Confirmed Czech market size (e.g. distributor registrations in the tens of thousands per year).
